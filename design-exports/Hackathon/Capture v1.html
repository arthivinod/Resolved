UX picks, on the screen

Inline rationale lives in capture-screen.jsx — search for // — UX:. This panel maps every brief question to the pick I made.

Q1 · Expand behavior

Accordion — one open at a time
Multi-open creates ambiguous commit-state and a 9-row buffet for stressed users.

Q2 · Chevron + border

180° in 200ms · saffron border on expand
Same easing curve as Home's CTA press for motion-language continuity.

Q3 · Sub-option tap

Immediate → Extracting
The OS picker IS the preview; the Extracting screen IS the confirm. A second step would burn demo time and contradict the "AI does the rest" promise.

Q4 · Multi-file?

Single only
Per anti-goals. Sub-option tap commits.

Q5 · Extraction failure

Inline, on the card
Toasts disappear; inline ties the message to the affordance that caused it. Tone: descriptive + actionable, never apologetic.

Q6 · Note limit?

Unlimited · soft 280 hint
Counter appears at 200 chars, goes amber past 280. Never blocks.

Q7 · Try-a-sample

Dashed-underline text link at the bottom
A chip competes with real cards; a floating button screams. Dashed = universal "demo / optional".

Q8 · Preview after pick?

No preview — commit-on-tap
Preview = soft confirm dialog = anti-goal friction.
