Q1 · Cursor

Blinks during stream, disappears on complete. Post-stream state reads as a finished document.

Q2 · Edit/Reword placement

Inline pill buttons below the card. ⋯ menus hide editorial controls; long-press is undiscoverable for first-time judges.

Q3 · Edit target

Bottom sheet with textarea. Inline-edit jumps layout; separate screen loses context.

Q4 · Regenerate

Named-direction sheet. Blind re-runs feel random. 4 directions cover ~90% of real edits.

Q5 · Corner bracket

Top-left only. Frame = decorative; signature mark = authorship without boxing.

Q6 · Format switcher

None on screen. Channel decided on Strategy; back chevron is one tap.

Q7 · CTAs by format

"File now" · "Post tweet" · "Start call" · "Submit filing". Each verb matches the medium's vocabulary.

Q8 · Helper line

Verb first, then what we do after — same arc per format. "escalation queued" reads as machinery-already-running.

Reword label

"Reword". Rejected "Regenerate" (jargon), "Try again" (implies failure), "New version" (bureaucratic).

Restore AI draft

Hidden until edited === true. Dashed-underline link, --text-tertiary. Subtle, not tempting.

Streaming interrupt

Tap-to-skip. Respects user time; cinematic-dialogue convention.

CTA loading

Pill morphs to inline spinner, label fades, 800ms hold, route. Keeps spatial anchor.
