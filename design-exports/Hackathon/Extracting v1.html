UX picks, on the screen

Inline rationale lives in extracting-screen.jsx — search for // — UX:. Three states delivered (A wired, B+C designed).

Q1 · Orb after loading

(b) Shrinks to 56pt next to heading
Disappearing erases the product moment; staying static wastes the upper third. Shrink = same object that moved = brand persistence.

Q2 · Field stagger

150ms each, first at 200ms
"Ledger filling itself" cadence. ~1.3s cumulative reveal — within demo patience.

Q3 · Confidence indicator

Binary ✓ / ? · dotted underline
"?" badge on inferred fields (e.g. Severity: High). No % meter — too engineering.

Q4 · Editability

Inline tap-to-edit
Whole row is the affordance. No pencil icon. Saffron focus ring while editing.

Q5 · Thumbnail placement

Full-width footer of the ledger card
Brief instinct was bottom-right at thumbnail size; I argued up — too easy to miss for a stressed user. Now it's part of the proof bundle.

Q6 · CTA copy

"Pick the play" + arrow
Brief draft "Confirm — pick the play" splits visually on a 56pt button. The act of tapping IS the confirm; the label names the next place.

Q7 · Loading duration

1.5s minimum hold
Sub-second extraction looks buggy. We don't slow real latency — just floor it.

Q8 · Transition out

Slide left on confirm
iOS-native forward nav.
